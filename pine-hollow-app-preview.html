The hard rules

  • No popups, no interstitials, no tracking, no sponsor content blocking app use. Ever.
  • Scorecard rankings are earned from real catch data — never bought, at any tier.
  • Sponsors never see private trap-location data. Reports are aggregated and anonymized.
  • Sponsored rows don't make the app pay-to-play — trappers can always type any product freehand.
  • Your branding is a template: logos and products swap through configuration, never destructive edits.

Calibrated against real print: even the smallest local weekly newspaper charges $100+/week ($400+/month) for a small ad — general readers, seen once. Tier 1 at $650/yr founding puts Pine Hollow in front of targeted trappers daily, all season, with a tap straight to your site.
